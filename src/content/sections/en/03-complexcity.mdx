Launched in 2012, ComplexCity is presented by UTC as one of the first research laboratories dedicated to smart-city issues. It brings together researchers from French Universities of Technology and Shanghai University, with academic and industrial partners.

The laboratory studies the city as a complex system. Its work combines engineering methods, data analysis, modelling, interpretation tools and humanities perspectives to understand today’s cities and design more resilient cities for the future.

Its application areas include risk and crisis management, urban metabolism, urban maintenance, energy efficiency, urban comfort, ageing and gerontology in the city.

### Industry and applied research

The official UTSEUS website also foregrounds industry relations. In this redesign, ComplexCity acts as that bridge: research projects are not shown as an abstract list, but as topics connected to real urban problems.

Urban logistics, building energy, safety, health, web data and digital culture help users understand how the institute connects engineering, territory and innovation.
