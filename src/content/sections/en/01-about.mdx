Created in 2005 by the French Universities of Technology network and Shanghai University, UTSEUS is a Sino-French engineering school built around training, research and international cooperation.

The institute trains more than 1,200 Chinese, French and European students each year in an international and multicultural environment. Its academic model connects the University of Technology of Compiègne, UTT and UTBM with Shanghai University.

Shanghai University was selected for its strong academic base, its location in one of Asia’s major economic regions and its proximity to French and European companies established in Shanghai.

[Visit the official UTSEUS website](https://utseus.shu.edu.cn/)

### What visitors should understand quickly

UTSEUS is not only an exchange programme. It is a shared structure connecting engineering education, applied research, intercultural experience and industry relations.

The official UTSEUS website highlights five complementary areas: **Education**, **Campus life**, **Industry**, **Alumni** and **News & Events**. This redesign follows that logic so users can move from discovery to practical opportunities.
