The original UTC page presents UTSEUS as a bridge between Chinese and French engineering education. This redesigned section turns that long description into four readable pathways.

Chinese students follow engineering tracks in computer engineering, mechanical engineering, biological engineering and materials science. French and European students can join exchange semesters that combine academic courses, intercultural immersion, company projects and innovation topics.

### Education

The page should help users distinguish the audiences: Chinese students building a Sino-French engineering pathway, and French or European students looking for an academic experience in Shanghai.

For better usability, the programmes below are presented as filterable cards instead of a continuous text block.

### Campus life

The UTSEUS experience also happens outside the classroom: student life in Shanghai, cultural adaptation, international teamwork, Shanghai University’s campus environment and collective projects.

This matters for UX because visitors are not only looking for a degree. They want to understand daily life, support conditions and the human value of the exchange.
